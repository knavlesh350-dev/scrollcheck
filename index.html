    About ScrollCheck

    Your guide to understanding digital habits

    What is ScrollCheck?

    ScrollCheck is a digital wellness tool that helps you understand how you spend your short-form video time. It's designed to help you make intentional choices about your screen time without judgment.

    Not All Screen Time is Equal

    ScrollCheck categorizes your usage into four types:

    • 🎓 Educational: Learning new skills or information
    • 🛠️ Useful: Practical or productive content
    • 🎭 Entertainment: For fun and enjoyment
    • ❓ Unclassified: Unclear or mixed content

    How It Works

    1. Enter your short-video usage for the day
    2. ScrollCheck analyzes your data
    3. Receive personalized feedback
    4. Track your progress and earn rewards
    5. Build healthier digital habits

    Scroll Balance Score

    The Scroll Balance Score is an educational behavioral indicator, not a medical diagnosis. It helps you understand your habits based on factors like:

    • Total scrolling time
    • Entertainment percentage
    • Late-night usage
    • Educational/useful content percentage

    🚀 Future Features

    AI Content Classification

    In a future version, ScrollCheck could estimate whether content is educational, skill-based, entertainment, or unclassified using available video metadata or transcripts and an AI classification system.

    Automatic Phone Tracking

    The current prototype uses user-entered data. A future Android version may use supported device usage APIs to collect usage information with your permission.

    Your Data

    ScrollCheck saves all your data locally on your device using browser storage. No data is sent to any server, and you have complete control over your information.

    Privacy & Honesty

    This is a student innovation project (NEEV) dedicated to helping you understand your digital habits. We clearly distinguish between what works now and what's coming in the future. We never pretend to have features we don't actually have.
